Why Trainer eval_loss is lower (4.52 vs 5.71)
---------------------------------------------
Not two different models. Two different ways of averaging the same per-batch
mean cross-entropy:

  - The model returns one scalar per batch: CrossEntropyLoss over valid tokens
    (ignore_index=-100), i.e. a mean over that batch's tokens.
  - Hugging Face Trainer then treats those batch scalars as roughly
    example/batch-equal: it repeats each batch loss by batch_size and takes
    the mean. Short Wikipedia paragraphs get almost the same weight as long ones.
  - evaluate_a2.py reweights by the number of valid next-token targets:

        CE_token = sum_b (loss_b * n_b) / sum_b n_b

    so a paragraph with 100 tokens does not count as much as one with 800.

Validation paragraphs vary a lot in length, so the two averages diverge.
PPL = exp(CE) then magnifies CE 4.52 vs 5.71 into 92 vs 301.

Use token-weighted PPL for the assignment and for the A1 comparison.
